Upadrava ( complication ) is disorder itself, big or small, manifesting in the
later period of a disease and rooted in the same. Upadrava is so named because it
appears after the disease ( manifests ). Thus disease is main ( or primary ) while
complication is secondary. The latter is often pacified when the main disease is
pacified. As it appears later it becomes more afflicting because of the patient being
already suffering from the disease. Hence one should overcome the complication
quickly, [ 40 ]
